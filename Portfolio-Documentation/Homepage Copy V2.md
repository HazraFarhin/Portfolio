## 08 / Field Notes (principle cards)

**Section label:** `Field Notes` **Heading:** `Six Things I Hold Onto, Regardless of Brief`

**01 / Friction is a design defect.**

> If a user has to think twice to complete a simple task, it's something to fix. Every screen is judged against how much effort it removes.

**02 / Interfaces that explain less.**

> A well-structured product doesn't need onboarding to carry the weight of bad design. Clarity comes from hierarchy and language.

**03 / Business goals and user needs get designed together, not traded off.**

> A product only works long term if it works for the business behind it too. Every decision gets weighed against what the user needs and what keeps the product viable, then checked with stakeholders and developers before it ships.

**04 / Accessible and compliant.**

> Designing for WCAG-level accessibility, real localisation including RTL, and data-conscious UI patterns happens at the wireframe stage.

**05 / Systems outlast screenshots.**

> Components, states, and documented logic get reused. A polished one-off mockup doesn't.

**06 / Tools change, judgment doesn't.**

> From Figma to AI-assisted design and rapid prototyping tools, the stack keeps shifting. I stay current on purpose, because the fastest path from concept to shipped interface keeps moving.

## 09 / Process (Operating Loop)

**Section label:** `How I Work` **Heading:** `The Loop`

```
Understand → Align → Structure → Design → Transfer
```

**Understand**

> Research the product, the users, and the real constraint, technical, business, or regulatory, that's actually shaping the problem.

**Align**

> Sit down with clients and developers early to confirm what's actually needed and what's actually buildable, before it's baked into a design nobody can ship.

**Structure**

> Turn findings into information architecture and flows the whole team, including developers and stakeholders, can agree on before a single pixel is final.

**Design**

> Build high-fidelity screens and interactive prototypes tested against real content and edge cases, not idealised data.

**Transfer**

> Document components, states, and logic, and hand off production-ready screens, so the system keeps working without me.
